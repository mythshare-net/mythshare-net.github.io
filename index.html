About MythShare

C:\MYTHSHARE> type README.TXT

===== README.TXT ==============================================

MythShare is dedicated to bringing the rich tapestry of Celtic and Welsh mythology to life through retro-inspired browser games. Each title is small, self-contained, and free to play — distributed in the spirit of the great shareware era.

Our games draw from ancient sources including:

  • The Mabinogion — Eleven prose stories from medieval Welsh manuscripts.
  • Celtic Folklore — Tales of the Tuatha Dé Danann, the Otherworld, and ancient heroes.
  • Welsh Legends — Stories of Annwn, the Tylwyth Teg, and the great bards of Britain.

Each game is crafted with respect for these traditions while embracing the nostalgic charm of classic gaming.

  • Authentic Lore Rooted in genuine Welsh mythology
  • Retro Style Classic gameplay, modern accessibility
  • Browser-Based Play instantly, no install required

The Lore

C:\MYTHSHARE\SCROLLS> dir

MABINOGION.TXT

The Mabinogion

The Mabinogion is the earliest prose literature of Britain. These tales include the Four Branches of the Mabinogi, native Welsh stories, and Arthurian romances passed down through medieval Welsh manuscripts.

ANNWN_____.TXT

Annwn — The Otherworld

In Welsh mythology, Annwn is the Otherworld, ruled by Arawn or Gwyn ap Nudd. It is a realm of eternal youth and abundance, home to the spectral Cŵn Annwn — the Hounds of Annwn that ride with the Wild Hunt.

TYLWYTHTEG.TXT

The Tylwyth Teg

The Tylwyth Teg, or "Fair Family", are the Welsh fairies. These beings inhabit lakes, rivers, and hills, and are known for their enchanting music, secret revels, and dangerous magical gifts.
